# 岛屿研究现场

Mode: Experience → Read → Operate. Audience: curious researchers moving from a question map into real material.
Scope: Generated island L1, six research families and stable settlement arrangements, building entrances and semantic research diagrams, L2 reading rooms and question sheets, mobile entry, GPU and SVG geometry, atlas departure/return. The bespoke machine-curiosity flow stays separate.
Job: Find a meaningful research entry, recognize its building, examine sources and conflicting explanations, carry a private question onward.
Primary interaction: choose a scene building, guide row or wayfinder address → approach the real building → inspect its semantic research-room diagram → explicitly enter any room or resume reading → carry a question into sources or a private research draft. Closing retains the selected entrance and restores its action focus. Previous-place return restores room and saved scroll; reading history holds at most 30 addresses and branches from the returned address when choosing a new room. Entrance previews add no history. Canvas picking uses the actual graph/camera, matching press/release and at most 6px total travel; drag/cancel does not activate. SVG keyboard entry follows the same entrance contract. Free access, no prerequisite visits or external rewards.
Direction: Extend the inherited mineral-paper isometric research atlas. Nine recognizable building functions carry family pigments and instruments: 留白庭, 观测台地, 共研书院, 接榫工坊, 模型回廊 and 生长庭院. Topic precedes broad cluster when choosing the family; stable island identity varies its entrance orientation while preserving semantic neighbors. A compact question/guide at left gives the island most of the view; a reading pane retains spatial context; phone reading becomes full screen. This is a generative family system, not individually authored scenes for every island.
Form: The desktop entrance uses min(430px,34vw), internal scrolling and sticky actions; mobile gives it natural page height and static actions. Court, stacks, forum and bench diagrams map actual content rooms, never physical floor counts. Their 20px door, 24px upper court corners and 22px lower forum corners are scoped component conventions, not global radius tokens or detector ignores. Question sheets use two columns with the main and expanded sheet spanning the width; phone sheets use one column. Paper, a light ambient shadow and brief clipped reveals support disclosure; reduced motion disables the reveals. Family character repeats in scene and portraits without replacing day/night action contrast. Mobile carried questions start collapsed with a one-line preview and source comparisons stack vertically. Night replay is hidden while at a building entrance or reading.
Proof: Wayfinder positions and coastline come from the shared scene graph. Six optional research-family itineraries suggest reading order, not scientific edges; every available building remains freely reachable. Source URLs, lossless content grouping and old floor-address compatibility remain authoritative. Question sheets retain existing source/author/rewrite context. Within the same island visit, station, room, scroll, question expansion, source selection, open desk and drafts survive return; island changes reset temporary state. The library carries the chosen question and labels references as island-level material without a recorded question-specific correspondence. Comparisons use only two existing references or digests, preserve links and disclose missing summaries. Private expectation, falsifying-observation and next-test fields are drafts, with no new AI execution or experiment. Explicit append transfers a draft into the existing browser-local note, preserving previous text, avoiding duplicates and rejecting overflow beyond 1200 characters while retaining note and draft. Workshop, gallery and unfinished-garden rooms reuse existing depth. Spatial proximity never establishes a scientific relationship. Instruments are architectural exhibits, editorial characters are not live people, and model experiments do not validate island claims.
Constraints: preserve source withdrawals, structure proposals, transplants, night replay, existing ledger semantics and lazy GPU loading. No new shipping raster assets.
Open: model-provider quality and user research remain separate from local interface validation. No deployment is part of this design turn.
